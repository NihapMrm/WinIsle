# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want WinIsle to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect different opinions, experiences and ways of working.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment in public or in private.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or see unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [NihapMrm](https://github.com/NihapMrm), through GitHub. Reports are handled privately, and the privacy of the person reporting is respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will review every report and may respond with a private warning, a request for a public apology, a temporary ban from the project, or, for serious or repeated violations, a permanent ban. Comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code may be edited or removed.
